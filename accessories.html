Accessories

Nine things. Bags, a belt, a hat, a scarf, socks, a bandana, a card holder and a repair kit. Every one of them is either cut and sewn in our Toronto studio or knitted by the same Manitoba workshop that makes the sweaters, and none of them carries a logo.

Accessories are also where most of our gift orders sit, so it is worth saying: everything on this page ships in the same unbleached cotton pouch, we will write a note by hand if you ask in the order notes, and we never include a price on a packing slip when the order is marked as a gift. If you would rather let somebody choose, gift cards are issued in USD and do not expire.

Leather, honestly

Two things on this page are leather: the belt and the card holder. Both are a by-product of the meat industry rather than a driver of it, both are vegetable tanned rather than chrome tanned, and both come from a tannery with a Leather Working Group gold rating, which audits water use and effluent treatment. If you would rather not buy leather at all, the weekender is available with cotton webbing corners instead of leather ones at no extra cost. Ask in the order notes. Nothing else in the range contains animal skin, and the collar on the bomber is knitted wool pile, not shearling, despite the name it has picked up.

How canvas ages

New 18 oz canvas is stiff, pale and slightly rough. Over the first three months it softens where it folds, darkens where you hold it, and picks up the shape of whatever you carry. It will also mark. Coffee, ink and bicycle grease all leave a trace on undyed cotton, and no amount of washing takes them fully out. We could dye it to hide that. We would rather it looked like it had been used, because it has.
