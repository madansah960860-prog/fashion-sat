Contact form

Everything marked required has to be filled in. If something is wrong you will get a plain message under the field telling you what to fix.

Your details We reply to this address and use it for nothing else. Only if you would rather we rang you. Format PXS-00000. It is at the top of your confirmation email.
Your message Twenty characters minimum. If it is about fit, tell us your height and the chest measurement of a garment you already like, and we can be much more useful.

What happens to what you send. Your message goes to our support inbox, hosted in Canada. It is kept for 24 months and then deleted. It is not added to the mailing list, is never sold, and is not shared with anybody outside the company except a courier, when the message is about a delivery. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Visiting the studio

You are welcome any time we are open, and you do not need to tell us you are coming. The full range is on the rail in every size and you can try all of it on.

  • Getting there. Dundas West subway station is a twelve-minute walk. The 505 Dundas streetcar stops at Sterling Road. There is free street parking on Perth Avenue.
  • Access. Level entry from the courtyard, a freight lift to the sixth floor, and one step at the unit door. Tell us in advance or ring the bell and somebody will put the ramp down. There is an accessible toilet on the floor.
  • Trying things on. There is one curtained changing area. If you would rather have the space to yourself, come on a Tuesday.
  • Repairs. Bring a garment in and we will look at it while you wait, though most repairs need to be left with us.

Press

We are a small company and we do not have a press office. Photographs, measurements and fibre content for every product are free to use, without asking, provided the garment is identified correctly and the price is quoted in USD. We do not send samples, and we do not pay for coverage. Write to hello@plaintextsupply.ca with "press" in the subject line.
