Cookie Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026. First published 15 January 2026.

Note. This policy is a starting template. It should be reviewed by a lawyer, and the table below verified against what the deployed site actually sets, before this site is used commercially.

The short version

  • Four strictly necessary items. They cannot be turned off because the site stops working without them.
  • Nothing else is set until you choose. Not one analytics cookie, not one advertising cookie.
  • "Reject optional" is exactly as easy to press as "Accept all". There is no dark pattern here and there is not going to be.
  • You can change your mind at any time with this link.

What a cookie is, briefly

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store and send back on the next request. It is how a website recognises that two page loads came from the same person. Related technologies, local storage and session storage, do a similar job without sending anything back automatically. We use both and we list both below, because a policy that only lists cookies and quietly uses local storage is being clever rather than honest.

How to change or withdraw consent

  1. Open the cookie preferences panel. Choose Accept all, Reject optional, or tick the individual categories and save. It takes effect immediately, with no page reload and no confirmation dialog.
  2. Or clear this site's data in your browser. That removes the stored choice and you will be asked again next visit.
  3. Or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break the basket and checkout, which is a genuine consequence rather than a threat.
  4. We honour a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of advertising and of the sharing of personal information, without you having to do anything else.

Blocking cookies in your browser

  • Safari, iPhone and iPad: Settings, then Safari, then Privacy and Security. "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking" is on by default and already blocks most third-party advertising cookies.
  • Safari, Mac: Safari menu, Settings, Privacy.
  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Third-party cookies.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security. Enhanced Tracking Protection is on by default.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions.

Private or incognito browsing discards everything when you close the window, including our necessary cookies, so you will see the banner again each time.

Cookies we do not use

  • No session recording, heatmapping or mouse-movement tracking.
  • No social network sharing pixels. There are no share buttons on this site, which is why.
  • No A/B testing or personalisation cookies. Everyone sees the same site.
  • No device fingerprinting.
  • No cookies at all on the legal pages beyond the strictly necessary ones, and no advertisement slots on those pages either.

Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a cookie we update the table above and the date at the top. If a new category is added, the consent banner will reappear and ask you again rather than assuming your previous answer covers it.
