Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated 3 September 2026. First published 15 January 2026.

Note. This page is a starting template. It should be reviewed by a lawyer, and checked against current United States Federal Trade Commission endorsement guidance and Canadian Competition Bureau guidance, before this site is used commercially.

1. Affiliate links: we do not use them

As of the date above, Plaintext Supply Co. earns no affiliate commission from anything on this website. There are no affiliate links, no referral links to other retailers, and no sponsored placements in the journal.

Where our writing mentions a product we do not sell — for example the list of eleven garments we do not make in A seasonless wardrobe in nineteen pieces — we deliberately do not link to a shop, precisely so that nobody has to wonder whether the recommendation was bought.

If that ever changes, we will do all of the following, and this paragraph is the commitment:

  • Disclose the relationship in plain language at the point of use, immediately next to the link, not only here at the bottom of the site.
  • Use wording a reader cannot miss, such as "This is an affiliate link. If you buy through it we are paid a commission."
  • Update this page with the date the arrangement started and who it is with.
  • Never let a commission determine what we recommend or how we describe it.

That is what FTC 16 CFR Part 255 requires of a material connection, and it is also what the Canadian Competition Bureau expects. We would rather commit to it in advance than write it after the fact.

2. Advertising on this site

Pages on this site other than the legal pages and the error page contain clearly labelled advertisement slots. Where those slots carry advertising:

  • Advertisements are supplied by third-party networks, which may include Google. We do not choose the individual advertisements.
  • An advertisement is not an endorsement. We do not vet advertisers and we have no relationship with them beyond the ad network.
  • Advertisement slots are visually distinct, are labelled "Advertisement", never sit above the main content of a page, and never touch navigation or a button.
  • Any dealing you have with an advertiser is between you and them. We are not a party to it and cannot help with it.
  • No advertisement is served before you consent to advertising cookies. See the cookie policy.

3. Endorsements, gifting and reviews

  • We do not pay for coverage, and we have never sent a free garment to anybody in exchange for a post. If you see our clothes on somebody, they bought them.
  • We do not run an ambassador programme.
  • There are no reviews or star ratings on this site. We have not built a system that can verify a reviewer bought the item, and publishing unverified reviews would be inviting trust we cannot support. If we add reviews we will state clearly how they are verified and we will not filter out the bad ones.
  • Our referral programme pays existing customers store credit for referring a friend. That is a material connection, it is disclosed on the FAQ page, and anyone using a referral code should assume the person who gave it to them earns credit.

4. Product information

  • Measurements are taken from garments laid flat, with a published tolerance of plus or minus half an inch. See the size guide.
  • Fibre content is as supplied by our mills and spinners. We publish their certifications, and where a material has no certification we say so instead of implying one. See the materials page.
  • Natural materials vary between lots. Undyed wool, boiled wool made from surplus yarn, and vegetable-tanned leather will differ in shade. This is described on the product pages and is not a fault.
  • Colour on your screen depends on your display. We do not colour-grade product photographs, and we cannot calibrate your monitor.
  • Photographs are supplied by photographers via Unsplash and show garments of the type described. They are illustrative.
  • Performance statements, such as how long a waxed jacket sheds rain, are based on our own informal testing and on customer reports, not on laboratory measurement. We say so where we make them, for example in this journal entry, and we do not quote a figure we have not measured.

5. General information only

The journal and the care guidance on this site are general information about clothing and textiles, written by people who make clothes. They are not professional advice of any kind.

  • Not financial advice. The pricing and margin figures published on our story page and in this journal entry describe our own business. They are not a template for yours and are not a recommendation.
  • Not legal advice. Nothing on this site, including the legal pages, is legal advice to you.
  • Not safety advice. Our re-waxing instructions involve warm wax. Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions on whatever product you buy. Do not heat paraffin over a naked flame. We are not responsible for what happens in your kitchen.
  • Care guidance is general. Washing machines and water differ. Follow the care label sewn into the garment, and if the label and this website ever disagree, the label wins.

6. Accuracy and availability

We try to keep this site accurate and current. We cannot guarantee that every page is free of error, that stock shown as available has not sold in the last few minutes, or that the site will be available without interruption.

Where we get something wrong in a journal entry we correct it in place and add a dated note saying what changed, rather than editing quietly. Where a price is obviously wrong we will contact you before dispatch, as set out in the terms of service.

7. External links

This site links to a small number of external pages: advertising opt-out tools, standards bodies, and our stockists. We do not control those sites, we are not responsible for their content, availability or privacy practices, and a link is not an endorsement of everything on the other end of it.

8. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, Plaintext Supply Co. is not liable for loss arising from reliance on general information published on this site. Our liability in connection with goods you buy from us is set out in the terms of service, and nothing on this page limits your rights under the consumer protection law of your province, territory or state.

9. Placeholder content on this site

Parts of this website are currently marked in the page source with an HTML comment reading "PLACEHOLDER — REPLACE WITH REAL VERIFIED INFORMATION BEFORE LAUNCH". Those items — business name, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, prices and certain figures — must be verified and replaced before this site is used commercially. Until that is done, treat them as illustrative rather than as statements of fact.
