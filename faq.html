Frequently Asked Questions

These are the questions that actually arrive in the inbox, in roughly the order of how often they arrive. Each one opens when you click on it, and you can open all of them at once with the button below.

Money and currency

Why are prices in US dollars if you are a Canadian company?

Because we sell to both countries and we did not want two price lists that drift apart every time the exchange rate moves. One number, quoted in USD, means a shopper in Vancouver and a shopper in Chicago are looking at the same figure for the same coat.

Practically, for a Canadian customer: your card issuer converts USD to Canadian dollars at its own rate on the day the payment settles, and most Canadian cards add a foreign transaction fee of around 2.5 per cent. We do not receive that fee. If your bank offers a no-foreign-fee card, use it.

What tax will I pay in Canada?

Canadian sales tax is added at checkout based on your shipping province: GST at 5 per cent, HST at 13 or 15 per cent, or GST plus provincial sales tax depending on where you are. The tax line is itemised on the receipt and on the invoice printed inside your parcel. Because we ship from within Canada there is no import duty and nothing to pay on delivery.

United States orders are shipped from Canada but our goods qualify as North American origin under CUSMA, so there is no duty to pay on delivery there either. Sales tax is collected where we are required to.

How do gift cards work?

Issued in USD in denominations of 50, 100, 200 and 500. They arrive by email with a code, or we will post a printed card in a cotton pouch at no extra charge if you ask. They never expire, they can be used across multiple orders until the balance runs out, and the balance is shown at checkout.

They are not redeemable for cash, in line with Ontario consumer protection rules, and if an order paid with a gift card is returned the refund goes back onto the gift card balance.

How does the referral programme work?

Ask us for a referral code, or find it in your order confirmation. The person you give it to gets 20 USD off their first order over 120 USD. You get 20 USD in store credit once that order has passed its 30-day return window.

We wait for the window to close because otherwise we would be paying credit on orders that come straight back, and that cost would end up in the price of everything. There is no cap on how many people you refer. Credits do not expire.

Do you ever sell seconds?

Yes, and it is the only way to buy from us below the list price. Seconds are garments with a genuine cosmetic fault: a skipped stitch, a colour mismatch between panels, a mark that would not come out. Each one is photographed close up with the fault visible and described in words, and sold at a fixed 40 per cent less.

There is no seconds page because there is rarely more than a handful. Ask to be added to the seconds list by emailing hello@plaintextsupply.ca and we email that list, and only that list, when there is something on it. Seconds are returnable like anything else.

When is the sale?

There is not one, and there never has been. The full reasoning, including the arithmetic, is in this journal entry. The short version is that our margin is about eight per cent, which is not enough to discount from, and that is a deliberate consequence of not building markdown into the price in the first place.

Sizing and fit

Which size am I?

Measure a garment you already own and like, flat on a table, armpit seam to armpit seam, then double it. Compare that number against the tables on the size guide. That is far more reliable than measuring your body, because it accounts for how much room you actually want.

If you would rather ask: tell us your height and the chest measurement of a garment you like, and we will tell you which size to take. If we get it wrong we pay for the exchange.

Can I order two sizes and send one back?

Yes, and we would rather you did than guess. Returns are free within Canada and the United States, so ordering two and keeping one costs you nothing. It is also cheaper for us than a return followed by a reorder.

Is this menswear or womenswear?

Neither. Everything is cut on one unisex block in XS to XXL. The US numeric equivalents on the size guide are there so you can translate from sizing you already know, not because there are two different fits.

I am tall. Will the sleeves be long enough?

Probably, at the larger sizes. Sleeve length grows about three quarters of an inch per size. Over 6 ft 2 in, take the larger of two sizes for the sleeve rather than the chest, and note that the fisherman rib and the cardigan have deep cuffs that can be worn turned down. Every sleeve measurement is published on the size guide.

Orders and delivery

How long will delivery take?

In-stock orders leave Toronto within one business day. After that: Ontario and Quebec two to three business days, the Prairies three to five, British Columbia four to six, Atlantic Canada three to five, the territories seven to fourteen. United States three to eight. Full detail, state by state and province by province, on the shipping page.

When is shipping free?

On orders over 150 USD to anywhere in Canada or the United States. Below that it is a flat 12 USD. International shipping is calculated at checkout and the customer is responsible for import duty and local tax, which we say before you pay rather than after.

What does made to order mean, and how long does it take?

Three pieces are cut or knitted individually rather than in a batch: the rain shell, the alpaca turtleneck and the weekender bag. Lead time is four to six weeks and that is a real number, not a hedge.

Your card is charged when you order, because we buy materials for your specific garment. You get an email the week yours is cut or cast on, and another when it ships. If we go past six weeks, one email cancels it for a full refund with no argument.

Something is sold out. When will it be back?

When the next lot of cloth or yarn arrives, which is usually six to ten weeks and occasionally longer. We do not run a notify-me box that never resolves; email us and a person will tell you the actual date we expect, or tell you honestly that we do not know yet.

Confirmed dates for upcoming runs are published on the new arrivals page and they are production dates agreed with the mill, not marketing dates.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, if it has not shipped. Email us with the order number as soon as you can. Once a label has been scanned we cannot recall it, but you can refuse delivery or return it free.

Returns and repairs

What is the returns window, and who pays?

Thirty days from delivery, unworn, with tags attached. We pay the return postage within Canada and the United States and send you a prepaid label. Exchanges for a different size ship free in both directions, once per order. International returns are at your cost. Full process on the returns page.

Do you extend returns over the holidays?

Yes. Anything bought between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January.

Will you repair something I bought years ago?

Yes, for as long as we can source the parts, and there is no labour charge. You pay materials and postage. That covers moth holes, worn elbows, zips, buttons, seams, pocket bags, belt shortening, canvas bag handles and re-waxing the parka.

Email a photograph to repairs@plaintextsupply.ca and you will get a quote and a realistic turnaround before you post anything. In practice that is two to four weeks, and longer in January.

What do I do with a garment that is genuinely worn out?

Send it back rather than binning it. We pay the postage within Canada and the United States and give you 25 USD in credit for anything we can use. Wool is shredded into fill, cotton becomes studio cloths, leather becomes card holders. Some of what comes back is unusable and we say so on the materials page rather than calling this closed-loop.

The company

Where is everything made?

Cut and sewn in our unit at 214 Sterling Road in Toronto. Knitwear is knitted and linked at a shared workshop in Toronto's east end by people from a family business that has been doing this since 1974. Cloth and yarn come from named mills in England, Italy, Japan, Taiwan and Türkiye, all listed on the materials page.

Why does this website look like this?

Because the specification is the point, and the design is there to make it readable rather than to impress you. The typefaces are already on your device, so no font files download and nothing shifts as the page settles. There is one accent colour and it only marks things that carry meaning: a price, a link, the button you are meant to press.

Motion is limited to states — a card lifting, a block settling in — and all of it switches off if your device asks for reduced motion. No carousel, no pop-up, no auto-playing video, no script watching you read. It loads quickly on a slow connection, works at 320 pixels wide, follows your system into dark mode and reads cleanly in a screen reader. Longer answer on our story page.

Why are there no reviews or star ratings?

Because we have not built a system that can verify a reviewer actually bought the thing. Publishing unverified reviews would be inviting you to trust something we cannot vouch for. When we can do it properly we will, and not before.

Can I visit?

Yes, Tuesday to Saturday, 11:00 to 18:00 Eastern, at 214 Sterling Road, Unit 6, Toronto. No appointment needed. The whole range is on the rail in every size. Access details are on the contact page.
