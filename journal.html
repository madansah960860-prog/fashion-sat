Journal

Three or four times a year one of us writes something down properly. It is usually because the same question has arrived by email enough times that a short reply has stopped being adequate. There is no publishing schedule, no content calendar and nothing here is written to rank for anything.

Everything is by a person who works in the building. Where an entry makes a factual claim about a material, the source is named in the text.

Subjects we get asked about most

  • Care. Practical washing and repair guidance sits on the category pages, not here: outerwear care and how to wash wool without ruining it.
  • Fit. The size guide has every garment measured flat in inches, plus how to measure something you already own.
  • Sourcing. Fibre by fibre, with certifications and the gaps, on the materials page.
  • Money. Where every hundred dollars of revenue goes is published on our story.

Corrections

If we get something wrong in a journal entry we correct it in place and add a dated note at the bottom of the piece saying what changed. We do not quietly edit. As of 3 September 2026 there are no outstanding corrections.

Writing to us

There are no comments on this site, mostly because moderating them properly is a job nobody here has time to do well. If you want to argue with something, email hello@plaintextsupply.ca or use the form on the contact page. If you make a point we had not considered, we will say so in the entry and credit you unless you would rather we did not.
