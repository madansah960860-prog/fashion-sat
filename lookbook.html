Lookbook

Shot on a Tuesday afternoon in April on Sterling Road and in the lane behind it. No studio, no lighting kit, no stylist, no retouching beyond straightening the horizon. Everyone in these photographs is somebody who works in the building: two of our knitters, our packer, the person who does the accounts, and a neighbour who agreed to stand in a coat for twenty minutes.

Every garment is named, priced and linked. Every person's height and the size they are wearing is printed underneath. That is the entire point of a lookbook and most of them leave it out.

A note on the photographs themselves: they are inconsistent. Some are cropped tight, some are not, one is slightly underexposed. We kept them that way because a set of images that all look identical has been worked on until it stopped being a record of anything.

Look one — the working coat

  • Cotton Duck Chore Coat — 215 USD, size L
  • Waffle Henley — 98 USD, size M
  • Bridle Leather Belt — 95 USD, size M

Worn by: 6 ft 0 in. The coat is one size up from what he would take over a shirt alone, because he wears it over knitwear all winter.

Look two — deep winter

  • Waxed Field Parka — 348 USD, size M
  • Fisherman Rib Sweater — 225 USD, size S
  • Wool Watch Cap — 58 USD, one size

Worn by: 5 ft 11 in. This is the combination the parka was cut around; the sweater is a size smaller than the coat because the rib is bulky.

Look three — indoors, cold building

  • Undyed Shetland Crew — 175 USD, size XS
  • Ribbed Wool Socks — 42 USD for three pairs, size S

Worn by: 5 ft 5 in. The Shetland is true to size; she takes XS in everything we make.

Look four — the layered middle

  • Lambswool Cardigan — 210 USD, size XS (left)
  • Cotton Fisherman Polo — 128 USD, size M (left)
  • Mock Neck Rib Knit — 140 USD, size S (right)
  • Lambswool Scarf — 110 USD, one size (right)

Worn by: left, 5 ft 6 in; right, 5 ft 8 in.
