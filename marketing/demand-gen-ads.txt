POLICY CHECKS APPLIED

No all-capital words other than the standard garment size codes XS and XXL,
which are size designations rather than emphasis. Confirm this reading against
current policy before running.
No exclamation marks, no repeated punctuation, no symbols, no emoji.
No reference to the advertisement itself and no instruction to click.
No superlatives, no unverifiable claims, no rankings.
No urgency, no scarcity, no countdowns, no invented statistics.
No prices, discounts, sales or promotional offers of any kind.
No competitor names, no designer or luxury house names, no trademarks.
No body shape, weight, slimming or figure-flattering language.
No telephone numbers.
Every line is written to stand alone, because Google mixes assets automatically.
