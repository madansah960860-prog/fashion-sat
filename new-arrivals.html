New Arrivals

Because the capsule is seasonless, "new" here means one of two things: a piece that has just been added to the permanent range, or a limited run that has just landed or is about to. There is no autumn collection and there will not be a spring one.

There is also no countdown clock anywhere on this page. If a run has not arrived yet, the date is printed and that is all. If something is sold out, it says sold out. We do not display stock counts, because a stock count is almost always used to pressure people rather than to inform them.

How a run actually works here

  1. We buy a lot of cloth or yarn. A lot is a fixed quantity; there is no option to reorder the same one because the fleece or the weave will be slightly different next time.
  2. We divide it by pattern and work out how many garments it makes. That number is the run size, and it is the number printed in the table above.
  3. Production is scheduled with the workshop, usually six to ten weeks out.
  4. On the release date the run goes on sale at nine in the morning Eastern. There is no early access, no password page and no queue.
  5. When it is gone it is gone. We do not restock the same lot, and we say so rather than showing a "notify me" box that never resolves.

Pre-order and made to order

Three pieces are made to order with a four to six week lead time: the rain shell, the alpaca turtleneck and the weekender bag. Your card is charged when you order, because we buy the materials for your specific garment. You get an email the week yours is cut or cast on, and another when it ships. If we slip past six weeks you can cancel for a full refund with one email and no argument.

We do not run pre-orders on the limited drops. Taking money for a garment that has not been made, on a date we cannot yet guarantee, is not a promise we are able to keep, so we do not make it.
