2. What we collect and why

When you buy something

  • Your name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone number if you give one. We need these to fulfil the order and to let the courier find you.
  • What you bought, in what size, and for how much. We need this for the order, for returns, for warranty and repair claims, and for our tax records.
  • Payment confirmation. We never see or store your full card number. The card details are entered directly into our payment processor. We receive only a token, the last four digits, the card brand and whether the payment succeeded.

When you contact us

  • Whatever you put in the form or the email: your name, email address, optionally a phone number and order number, and the content of your message. Used to answer you, and for nothing else.

When you join the mailing list

  • Your email address and the date and time you consented. Under Canada's anti-spam legislation we are required to be able to demonstrate consent, so we record it.
  • Whether you opened an email and whether you followed a link in it. You can turn this off by replying to any email and asking.

When you make a wholesale enquiry

  • Shop name, your name, email, optional phone, city, country, website, and what you wrote. Used to assess and answer the enquiry.

Automatically, when you use this website

  • Server logs: your IP address, the pages requested, the time, the referring page and your browser's user-agent string. This is standard web server behaviour and it is used for security, fraud prevention and diagnosing faults.
  • Your cookie choice, if you have made one. Stored in your browser, not on our servers.
  • If, and only if, you consent to analytics: aggregated statistics about which pages are visited and roughly where visitors are, at city level.
  • If, and only if, you consent to advertising: identifiers used by advertising vendors, described in section 5.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • We do not require an account to buy anything.
  • We do not ask for your date of birth, gender, income or body measurements. If you volunteer a measurement in an email to get fit advice, it stays in that email thread.
  • We do not buy personal information from data brokers, and we do not append or enrich our records from external sources.
  • We do not fingerprint your browser or device.
  • There is no session recording, heatmapping or mouse tracking on this site.

4. Cookies and tracking

The cookie policy lists every cookie by name, purpose and lifetime. In summary:

  • Strictly necessary. Your basket, your language, your cookie choice, and a security token. These cannot be switched off because the site would not function, and they are set without consent as permitted by law.
  • Analytics. Off unless you turn them on. Nothing analytic loads before you press Accept or tick the analytics box.
  • Advertising. Off unless you turn them on. Nothing advertising-related loads before you consent.

The consent banner appears on your first visit, and Reject is exactly as easy to press as Accept. You can change your mind at any time using this link, which reopens the preferences panel, or by clearing site data in your browser.

5. Google, advertising and how to opt out

This site is built to be able to carry advertising, and pages other than the legal pages and the error page contain clearly labelled advertisement slots. Where advertising is served, it may be served by Google.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info, the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page, or YourAdChoices Canada.
  • Where Google Analytics is used with consent, you can prevent it entirely with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google's own handling of data is described in the Google privacy and terms page for partner sites.

None of this happens until you have consented to advertising cookies. If you press Reject optional, no advertising identifier is set by us and no advertising vendor script is loaded by this site.

8. Security

  • The whole site is served over HTTPS.
  • Card numbers never reach our servers.
  • Access to customer records is limited to the people who need it, with individual accounts and two-factor authentication.
  • Backups are encrypted at rest.
  • If a breach occurs that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and notify affected people as required by law, and we will tell you what happened plainly rather than in euphemism.

No system is perfectly secure and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, consistent with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). You must be 16 or over to make a purchase or subscribe to the mailing list, and 18 or over to enter into a wholesale agreement.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, write to privacy@plaintextsupply.ca and we will delete it promptly. We do not knowingly serve personalised advertising to anyone under 18.

10. Canadian rights: PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25

Most of our customers are in Canada, and our handling of personal information is governed by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), together with provincial legislation where it applies.

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Know how it is used and who it has been disclosed to.
  • Have inaccurate information corrected.
  • Withdraw consent, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. Note that withdrawing consent to hold your order record would prevent us from handling a return or warranty claim.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you are not satisfied with our answer.

Quebec residents. Under Quebec's Law 25 you additionally have the right to data portability, the right to ask that personal information be de-indexed or ceased to be disseminated in defined circumstances, and the right to be informed when a decision about you is made exclusively by automated processing. We do not make any decision about you by automated means. Our privacy officer is identified in section 1.

Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have their own private sector privacy legislation which may give you additional rights. We honour requests under those statutes in the same way.

We respond to requests within 30 days at the latest and usually within five business days. There is no charge.

11. California rights: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. The categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to exceptions such as completing a transaction or meeting a legal obligation.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not give you worse prices, worse service or a worse selection for exercising any of these rights, and we do not operate any financial incentive programme tied to personal information.

Categories of personal information collected in the last twelve months, in CCPA terms: identifiers (name, email, postal address, IP address); customer records (billing and shipping address, phone number); commercial information (products purchased); internet activity (pages viewed, referring page); and geolocation at city level only, and only where analytics consent was given.

We do not sell personal information for money. If you consent to advertising cookies, the resulting disclosure of advertising identifiers to advertising vendors may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA, and you can opt out at any time.

You may make a request through an authorised agent. We will need to verify your identity, which we do by matching against the email address on your order record.

12. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

This section is the destination of the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in the footer of every page.

Plaintext Supply Co. does not sell personal information for money, and never has. We also do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising unless you have actively consented to advertising cookies.

To opt out, do any one of the following. Any of them is sufficient and you do not need an account.

  1. Open the cookie preferences panel and either press "Reject optional" or untick "Advertising" and save. This takes effect immediately in your browser.
  2. Email privacy@plaintextsupply.ca with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share" and the email address on your order, if you have one. We will action it within 15 business days and confirm in writing.
  3. Send a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser. We treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request for the browser that sends it, and we do not require you to do anything else.
  4. Write to the postal address in section 1, marked "Privacy".

An opt-out does not stop you seeing advertising; it stops advertising being personalised using identifiers associated with your visits.

13. United Kingdom and European Union: UK GDPR and GDPR

We ship internationally, so some visitors are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. If you are, the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR applies to our handling of your personal information and you have the following rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

The legal bases we rely on are listed in section 3. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom. We would rather you told us first, at privacy@plaintextsupply.ca, and we will answer within one month.

14. International transfers

We are in Canada. Canada has been recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred from the EEA to Canadian organisations subject to PIPEDA.

Some of our service providers are in the United States. Where personal information is transferred there, we rely on the relevant contractual protections offered by those providers, including standard contractual clauses where applicable. Canadian customers should be aware that information held by a United States provider may be accessible to United States authorities under their law.

15. Email and CASL

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation requires express consent before sending commercial electronic messages. So:

  • We ask you to tick a box, and then to confirm by replying to a confirmation email. Two steps, deliberately.
  • Every email we send identifies Plaintext Supply Co. as the sender and carries our full postal address, which also satisfies the United States CAN-SPAM Act for any subscribers there.
  • Every email has a working unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately and in any event within ten business days.
  • Transactional messages about an order you placed are not marketing and are sent regardless of marketing consent. You cannot unsubscribe from a dispatch confirmation, because you need it.
  • We send roughly one marketing email a month. We do not send abandoned basket emails, we do not send birthday emails, and we do not rent, sell or share the mailing list.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material we will update the date at the top, note what changed in a line at the bottom of this section, and, for a change that affects how we use information you have already given us, email everyone on the mailing list before it takes effect.
