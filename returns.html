Returns and Exchanges

Thirty days from delivery. Unworn, tags attached. We pay the return postage within Canada and the United States. Exchanges ship free both ways, once per order.

That is the whole policy. Everything below is detail, not conditions we are hoping you will not read.

How to return something

  1. Tell us. Use the form below, or email returns@plaintextsupply.ca with your order number. You do not need to give a reason, though it helps us if you do.
  2. We email you a prepaid label within one business day, for Canadian and United States addresses. Print it, or show the QR code at the counter if your carrier accepts one.
  3. Repack it. Use the same kraft mailer if it survived, or any box. The cotton pouch belongs to you either way.
  4. Drop it off at any accepting location. Keep the receipt until the refund lands.
  5. We inspect and refund. Refunds are issued within two business days of the parcel arriving. Your bank then takes three to ten business days to show it, which is out of our hands.

How to exchange for a different size

  1. Tell us which size you want instead when you request the return.
  2. If it is in stock we reserve it immediately and ship it the same day the return label is scanned, so you are not waiting twice.
  3. If it is out of stock we will tell you when it is expected and let you choose between waiting and a refund.
  4. Both legs are free, once per order. A second exchange on the same order means you cover the return leg; we still cover sending the replacement.

What we can and cannot accept

We can accept

  • Anything unworn with its tags attached, within 30 days of delivery.
  • Something tried on indoors. Obviously. Trying it on is the point.
  • A garment washed once by mistake, if it is otherwise unworn. We will not be difficult about it.
  • Made-to-order pieces, within the same 30 days, on the same terms. This is more generous than most made-to-order policies and it is deliberate.
  • Seconds and reduced-fault items.
  • Gifts. The person who received it can return it for store credit at the price paid, without the buyer being told.

We cannot accept

  • Anything visibly worn outdoors, washed repeatedly, altered or repaired by somebody else.
  • Anything returned after 30 days, unless it is faulty, in which case the window is irrelevant, see below.
  • Gift cards. They never expire, so there is nothing to refund.
  • The repair kit once the yarn pack has been opened.

Holiday extension

Anything bought between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January. No code, no request, it applies automatically.

Faulty goods, and your rights

The 30-day window is a returns policy for changing your mind. It is separate from, and does not limit, your legal rights when something is faulty or not as described.

If a garment fails because of how it was made rather than how it was used, we will repair it, replace it or refund it, and we will pay the postage both ways. That applies well beyond 30 days: seams, stitching, zips, hardware and linings are the things we made and we stand behind them. In practice we have never refused a genuine manufacturing fault regardless of age.

Normal wear is not a fault. Pilling on wool, fading on denim, scuffing and lightening on waxed cotton, and softening on canvas are all things we describe in advance on the product pages because they are expected behaviour of those materials. Those we will repair under the repairs programme at cost of materials, not refund.

Nothing on this page affects your statutory rights under the consumer protection legislation of your province or state, including the Ontario Consumer Protection Act, the Quebec Consumer Protection Act, or equivalent legislation where you live.

Refund detail

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot send them anywhere else.
  • Sales tax is refunded in full with the goods.
  • Outbound shipping is refunded if you return the entire order, and not refunded if you keep part of it.
  • If an order paid partly with a gift card or credit is returned, the credit portion goes back onto the balance and the rest to the card.
  • Referral credit is only paid once the referred order clears its 30-day window, so a returned order does not generate a credit.
  • Currency: refunds are issued in USD for the exact amount charged. If the exchange rate has moved, your bank may convert back to Canadian dollars at a different rate than when you paid. That difference is between you and your bank and we cannot adjust for it.
