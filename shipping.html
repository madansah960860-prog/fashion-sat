Shipping and Delivery

Everything ships from our unit at 214 Sterling Road in Toronto. We pack Monday to Friday. Orders placed before 14:00 Eastern on a business day generally go out the same afternoon; everything else goes the next business day. We do not ship on Ontario statutory holidays.

The 150 USD threshold is calculated on the value of the goods before tax and after any credit is applied. There is no express or overnight option. We ship by ground within North America because it is a fraction of the emissions of air freight, and the trade-off is that it is slower. That trade is deliberate and we would rather explain it than hide it behind a spinner.

Tax and duty

Canada

Canadian sales tax is added at checkout according to your shipping province, itemised on the receipt and on the invoice printed inside the parcel. Because the parcel never leaves the country there is no import duty and nothing to pay on delivery.

Tax rates are set by government and can change. The rate charged at checkout is the rate in force on the day you order.

United States

Our goods are made in Canada from North American and imported materials and qualify as originating under the Canada-United States-Mexico Agreement, so there is no duty for you to pay on delivery. Sales tax is collected at checkout in the states where we are registered to collect it. Nothing is ever collected by the courier at your door.

Everywhere else

International customers are responsible for import duty, VAT or GST and any courier handling fee in their own country. These are charged by your customs authority, not by us, and we have no way to estimate them accurately for every destination. We declare the full value of the goods on the customs form. We will not mark a parcel as a gift or under-declare its value; that is fraud and we will not do it however politely you ask.

Made-to-order lead times

Three items are made individually and do not ship in one business day:

  • Three-Layer Rain Shell — four to six weeks
  • Alpaca Turtleneck — four to six weeks
  • Canvas Weekender Bag — four to six weeks

You are emailed the week yours is cut or cast on, and again when it ships. If an order contains both stock and made-to-order items we ship the stock items immediately at no extra charge rather than holding the whole order.

Tracking

You get a tracking number by email the moment the label is scanned by the courier, not when it is printed. If the tracking has not moved in three business days, tell us and we will chase it; you do not have to.

If something goes wrong

  • Parcel marked delivered but not there. Check with neighbours and with the building first, then tell us. If it has not turned up within three business days of the delivery scan we will send a replacement or refund you. We do not require a police report.
  • Parcel lost in transit. If tracking has not updated for seven business days we treat it as lost, replace it, and deal with the courier ourselves.
  • Damaged in transit. Photograph the box and the contents and email us. Replacement goes out the same day we see the photograph.
  • Wrong address entered. Tell us before it ships and we will change it. After it ships we cannot redirect it, but the parcel will come back to us and we will resend at no charge.
  • Refused or unclaimed. Returned parcels are refunded less the outbound shipping cost we actually paid.

Packaging

Garments ship in an unbleached cotton pouch inside a recycled kraft paper mailer sealed with paper tape. No plastic mailers, no polybags, no tissue, no stickers, no branded box. The invoice is printed inside the mailer flap. If the order is marked as a gift the prices are omitted.
