Shop All

Twenty-six pieces. That is the entire range, not a selection from it. Eight items of outerwear, nine knits and nine accessories, all of them made to be worn together and none of them tied to a season. Prices are in United States dollars. Everything ships from our studio in Toronto.

Each entry below carries the same five facts: what it is made of, how it fits with real measurements, how to wash it, how the sizing runs, and the height and size of the person in the photograph. If you would rather read the measurements as a table, they are all on the size guide.

Free shipping across Canada and the United States on orders over 150 USD. Flat 12 USD below that. Thirty-day returns with a prepaid label. See shipping and returns.
