Stockists and Wholesale

Most of what we make is sold on this website. A small amount goes to nine independent shops in Canada and the United States, chosen because we have been in them, we know who runs them, and they were prepared to agree to our one condition: they do not discount the range. That condition exists so that the price on this site is the price everywhere, which is the whole argument in this journal entry.

Wholesale terms, published

Most brands make you fill in a form before they will tell you the terms. We would rather you knew whether it works for you before you spend twenty minutes on an enquiry, so here they are.

What we ask of a stockist

  • No discounting, ever, including seasonal sale periods and third-party marketplace listings.
  • Display the fibre content and care instructions with the garment. We supply printed cards.
  • Tell customers the truth about fit. We will train your staff over a call.
  • Send repairs back to us rather than turning customers away. We pay the freight in both directions.
  • No listing on Amazon, eBay or similar. This is in the agreement.

What we do not ask

  • No fixtures purchase, no branded furniture, no signage requirement.
  • No minimum annual spend after the opening order.
  • No marketing contribution and no listing fee.

Wholesale enquiry

If the terms above work for you, tell us about the shop. A person reads every one of these and you will get an answer within five business days, even if the answer is no.
